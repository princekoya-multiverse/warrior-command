The Switch Process

No direct "convert co-op to society" exists in BC law. You dissolve the co-op and register a new society. Or simpler: just register the society now, dissolve the co-op later.

StepActionCostTimeline
1Check society name availability — BC Registry Online$301 day
2File society incorporation — Submit constitution + bylaws under Societies Act$1001-2 weeks
3Society exists! — Now you can apply for grants. Dissolve co-op at your leisure.$0Immediate
4Pass special resolution — 75% member vote to dissolve co-op$01 month
5File voluntary dissolution — Submit affidavit + resolution to BC Registry~$302-4 weeks
6Transfer assets — Move co-op assets to the society$0Same day

⚡ TOTAL: ~$160 — Do Step 1 and 2 THIS WEEK. Everything depends on it.

Bylaws — Key Clauses for Grant Eligibility

Your constitution (purposes) must include these exact phrases for CRA charitable status later:

⎯ "To relieve poverty by providing health services and equipment to persons with disabilities"
⎯ "To advance education by producing documentary media about chronic illness and recovery"
⎯ "To promote health through PEMF therapy and related wellness protocols"
⎯ "To provide community health services to underserved populations"

CRA Charitable Status — The Golden Ticket

Once the society is established, apply for CRA registration. This lets you issue tax receipts for donations — the single biggest unlock for corporate sponsorships and major donations.

  • Timeline: 6-12 months after society registration
  • Corporations can write off 100% of donations
  • Individuals get 50%+ tax credits
  • Unlocks foundation grants (Vancouver Foundation, etc.)
